Creative Technology

How AI Is Part of My Creative Process

AI-assisted tools are part of most of my creative projects, including web development, storytelling, music, visual development, research, and content creation.

Depending on the project, I may use AI for brainstorming, research, coding assistance, writing and story development, visual exploration, audio and music production, or content refinement.

I remain responsible for the creative direction, structure, decisions, editing, review, and final presentation of my work. I evaluate and refine AI-assisted output rather than treating it as automatically finished.

I believe transparency is important. When AI contributes to a project, I prefer to acknowledge its role rather than present the work as entirely human-produced.
