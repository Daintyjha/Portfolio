About Me

A Journey Built on Creativity, Connection, and Curiosity

I am a hospitality professional, frontend developer, and creative storyteller based in Denmark. My passion has always been creating meaningful experiences — whether through welcoming people, designing digital spaces, writing stories, or developing creative projects.

My background in International Hospitality Management taught me the importance of communication, attention to detail, and understanding people's needs. Through hospitality, I developed strong problem-solving skills and learned how small details can create memorable experiences.

My curiosity later led me into technology and digital creation. I discovered the joy of building websites, exploring user experiences, and transforming ideas into interactive digital solutions.

Today, I combine my hospitality mindset, technical skills, and creativity to create work that is functional, meaningful, and human-centered.
